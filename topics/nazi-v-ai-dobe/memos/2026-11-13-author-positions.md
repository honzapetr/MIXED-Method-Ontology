# The author speaks from two positions

The author is the founder of an AI and innovation event, and the book presents itself as a philosophical essay on
human identity and meaning that explicitly refuses to be a guide to AI efficiency. He may therefore speak both as
an insider of the AI and innovation scene and as a humanist essayist.

For SKAD this points to the speaker position: from where the discourse speaks, with what legitimacy, and how the
two positions relate in the text. To check against the text and the context sources once they are read; it is an
impression from the framing of the book, not a finding.
