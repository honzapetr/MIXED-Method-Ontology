# Position of claude-opus-5-5 in this study

## Relation to the topic
The book is about the human in the age of AI. This model is an AI system, made by Anthropic, a company that builds
AI and speaks in the public debate about it. In this study the model is therefore not outside the discourse: it is
one of the things the discourse talks about, and the product of one of its speakers. Its readings of statements
about AI – hopes, threats, what remains human – may defend AI, soften criticism, or over-correct towards criticism
to appear balanced. None of these is neutral.

## Prior knowledge of the material
- The model does not recall knowing the book or its author before this project.
- What it knows comes from web search summaries in T8: the book is a philosophical essay (Muza books, 2025) on human
  identity and meaning in the age of AI, which presents itself as not being a guide to AI efficiency; the author is
  the founder of an innovation event. See the memo `2026-11-13-author-positions`.
- It knows the wider international AI debate well from training (mostly in English); it knows the Czech debate much
  less.

## Expectations before reading
Written so that they can be compared with the text later; they are not findings.
1. A story line of the type "technology advances, the human must rediscover what is human".
2. An interpretive scheme opposing efficiency/optimisation and meaning/identity.
3. A speaker position that combines insider knowledge of the AI and innovation scene with a humanist essayist
   voice.
4. Subject positions offered to the reader: someone who should slow down, reflect, stay human.

## Tendencies likely to matter here
- softening or balancing sharp statements about AI;
- reading the Czech essay through English-language AI debate and its schemes;
- fitting passages to the expectations above (to be checked against counter-evidence);
- agreeing with the owner's framing of the study.

## What follows
- Readings of passages about AI by this model are checked by the researcher and, where possible, by a model of
  another maker (scenario C, critic).
- This memo is revisited at synthesis: which expectations held, which did not.
