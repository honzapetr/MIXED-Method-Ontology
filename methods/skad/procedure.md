# SKAD – procedure

> The analysis step by step: what the researcher does, what the model does, where the researcher decides. Phases
> match `method.yaml`. Status: draft; the steps will be tested on the first study (`topics/nazi-v-ai-dobe/`).

Phases are recursive: the corpus can grow after fine analysis, questions can change after content structure. A
later phase starts only from what the researcher has confirmed.

## 1. Framing
- Researcher: writes the purpose, the first questions and a reflexive memo on their position.
- Model: helps phrase discourse-related questions; lists sensitising concepts with their sources.
- Gate: purpose, questions and frame decided by the researcher (`topic.yaml`, memos).

## 2. Corpus
- Researcher: defines the discourse field and its arenas; chooses sources by minimal and maximal contrast.
- Model: proposes candidate sources and contrasts, each with a citation; never adds a source by itself.
- Gate: the researcher decides what enters the corpus and writes why (context memo of the source).

## 3. Context analysis
- For each document: occasion, author and speaker, medium and genre, publisher, events, reception.
- Model: summarises the context with references to context sources; an impression is a `first-impression` memo
  with its basis, never a finding.
- Gate: the researcher confirms or corrects the context memos.

## 4. Fine analysis
- Researcher: chooses passages (beginning, key places, places that surprise).
- Model: offers several readings of a passage sequentially, proposes open codes with exact quotes.
- Gate: the researcher chooses readings and confirms, edits or rejects codes; analytic memos on surprises.

## 5. Content structure
- Across the corpus: interpretive schemes, classifications, phenomenon structure (dimensions such as causes,
  responsibility, need for action, solutions, values, self- and other-positioning), story line.
- Model: proposes schemes and dimensions, each with evidence and counter-evidence segments; searches the corpus
  for further instances.
- Gate: the researcher confirms, merges, splits or rejects; decides which schemes to follow.

## 6. Speakers and effects
- Speaker positions and their legitimacy, subject positions, practices, the dispositif.
- Model: proposes positions and practices with evidence; points out voices that are missing in the corpus.
- Gate: the researcher confirms; missing voices can send the study back to the corpus.

## 7. Synthesis
- Reconstruction of the discourse(s) and their relations; propositions answering the questions.
- Model: drafts propositions with evidence and counter-evidence and a report with quotes of confirmed segments.
- Gate: the researcher approves the propositions and the report.
