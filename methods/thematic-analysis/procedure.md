# Reflexive thematic analysis – procedure

> The six phases step by step: what the researcher does, what the model does, where the researcher decides.
> Phases match `method.yaml`. The phases are recursive, not a line. Status: draft, not yet tested on a study.

## 1. Familiarisation
- Researcher: reads the data, notes first impressions; writes a reflexivity memo.
- Model: summarises sources and lists notable passages with quotes, marked as aids to reading.
- Gate: the researcher writes the familiarisation memos.

## 2. Coding
- Researcher: codes the whole dataset systematically, semantic and latent codes.
- Model: proposes semantic and latent codes per segment with exact quotes.
- Gate: the researcher confirms, edits or rejects codes.

## 3. Generating initial themes
- Researcher: clusters codes into candidate themes around central organising concepts.
- Model: proposes candidate themes with their central organising concept and supporting codes.
- Gate: the researcher chooses candidate themes.

## 4. Developing and reviewing themes
- Check themes against the coded extracts and the whole dataset.
- Model: points out weak, overlapping or thin themes with evidence; looks for data that do not fit.
- Gate: the researcher merges, splits or drops themes.

## 5. Refining, defining and naming themes
- Model: drafts names and definitions.
- Gate: the researcher approves names and definitions.

## 6. Writing up
- Model: drafts the report with quotes of confirmed segments.
- Gate: the researcher edits and approves the report.
