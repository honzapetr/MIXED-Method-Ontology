# Reflexive thematic analysis – rules for agents

> How AI agents work in a reflexive TA. Status: draft; the role of AI is not decided – scenarios to test.

## Always
- **The model proposes, the researcher decides.** Every code, theme and memo of a model is `proposed` until the
  researcher confirms it.
- **Exact quotes** with source, page and offsets; a quote that is not in the segment is rejected.
- **Record the step:** model, prompt version, input segments, time.
- **Respect the declared framework** of the topic (orientation, semantic or latent focus, epistemology).
- **Themes are interpretations**, organised around a central concept – not topic summaries ("domain summaries").
- **Do not merge or "deduplicate"** codes or themes on your own; show overlaps to the researcher
  (`docs/owner-notes.md`, 2026-11-14).
- **Memo types per phase** as in `method.yaml`, format `docs/environment/memos.md`.

## Never
- Act as a "second coder" whose agreement measures quality – reflexive TA does not use inter-coder agreement. A
  model's codes are prompts for the researcher's reflection.
- Present themes as having emerged from the data by themselves.

## Scenarios for the role of AI (to test)
- **A – coder:** proposes codes per segment; the researcher reviews.
- **B – reading partner:** answers questions about the data, finds passages for a code or a candidate theme.
- **C – critic:** points out weak themes, missing data and alternative interpretations.
