# Reflexive thematic analysis – reflexivity

> What reflexivity means in reflexive TA, for the researcher and for the model. Written for reflexive TA only;
> overlaps with `methods/reflexivity.md` and other methods are kept on purpose (`methods/README.md`). Status: draft
> (T10), from summaries; to check against Braun & Clarke (`sources.md`).

## Stance

- In reflexive TA the researcher's subjectivity is a **resource**, not a bias to be removed. Themes are generated
  by the researcher from the data; they do not "emerge". Quality comes from a thoughtful, reflexive and deep
  engagement with the data, not from agreement between coders.
- Preconceptions are not bracketed; they are examined and owned. The reflexivity journal runs through all six
  phases.
- Reflexivity covers the researcher's theoretical assumptions too (orientation, e.g. experiential or critical;
  the framework that decides what counts as a theme).

## The model in a reflexive TA study

- A model cannot hold the position reflexive TA asks for: it has no subjectivity to own. Its proposals are
  material for the researcher's interpretation, never themes on their own. Some authors argue that GenAI use is not
  congruent with reflexive TA at all; others propose transparent, limited use (`sources.md`). Which uses are
  allowed in this environment is decided by testing the scenarios in `agent-rules.md`.
- Inter-coder agreement between the researcher and a model is not a quality criterion, as with human coders.
  Disagreement between them is material for the reflexivity journal.
- A model tends to produce topic summaries ("domain summaries") instead of themes with a central organising
  concept; this is checked at each gate.

## By phase

| Phase | Researcher | Model |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| familiarisation | reflexivity journal opens: position, expectations, reactions to the data | declares its prior knowledge and expected topics before reading |
| coding | which meanings do I code and which do I pass over? | notes semantic vs. latent codes; notes merges it did not make |
| generating-themes | are these themes or topic summaries? whose story? | flags its candidate themes that are topic summaries |
| reviewing-themes | did the model's framing become mine? | gives counter-readings of the themes |
| defining-themes | what the names say about my position | – |
| writing-up | reflexivity written into the report, including the role of the model | its memos and their status are reported |
