# Reflexive thematic analysis – research design

> How reflexive thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke) links the purpose of a study, its research questions and its
> theory. Written for reflexive TA only; overlaps with other methods are kept on purpose (`methods/README.md`).
> Status: draft from secondary summaries – to be checked against the works in `sources.md`.

## What reflexive TA is for

Developing themes – patterns of shared meaning organised around a central organising concept – through recursive
phases, with the researcher's subjectivity as a resource, not a bias. Braun & Clarke distinguish it from
coding-reliability TA (codebook agreed in advance, agreement between coders measured) and from codebook TA; in
reflexive TA codes are not checked for agreement between coders.

## Purpose and goals

To give an interpretive account of patterns of meaning in the data that answers the research question. TA is a
method, not a methodology: the study must state its own theoretical framework.

## How research questions arise

The question can be broad at the start (experiences, views, representations, practices) and may change during
the analysis as the researcher's understanding develops. The change is recorded.

## Role of prior theory

An explicit, declared framework: ontology and epistemology (e.g. critical realist, constructionist), inductive or
theory-led (deductive) orientation, semantic or latent focus. The framework is chosen by the researcher and
written into a reflexivity memo; it shapes what counts as a theme.

## Reasoning

Inductive or theory-led, always interpretive. Themes are generated by the researcher, they do not "emerge".

## Theory output

A small set of defined themes, each with a central organising concept, supporting codes and quoted extracts, and
an analytic narrative. A theory is not required; themes may feed a theory in a later study.

## Quality criteria

Braun & Clarke's checklist (thorough coding, themes that are coherent and distinct, analysis that interprets
rather than paraphrases, fit between the framework and the claims); reflexivity throughout. Inter-coder agreement
is not a quality criterion in reflexive TA.

## Working with the result

New data can be coded with the existing themes in mind, but the themes stay open to revision; a theme that no
longer holds is reworked, not forced.
