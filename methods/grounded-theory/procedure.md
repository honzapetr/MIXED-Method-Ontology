# Grounded theory – procedure

> The analysis step by step: what the researcher does, what the model does, where the researcher decides. Phases
> match `method.yaml` (Straussian naming; a constructivist study calls them initial, focused and theoretical
> coding). Status: draft, not yet tested on a study.

Data collection and analysis alternate: after each round of coding the researcher decides what data come next
(theoretical sampling). Memos are written in every phase.

## 1. Open coding
- Researcher: chooses the data of the round; codes line by line or incident by incident.
- Model: proposes codes per segment with an exact quote and a short definition; in-vivo codes keep the words of
  the data; process codes use gerunds.
- Gate: the researcher confirms, merges or rejects codes; writes memos on surprises.

## 2. Axial coding
- Researcher: relates codes to categories.
- Model: proposes categories and relations (causal condition, context, intervening condition, strategy,
  consequence, property), each with evidence segments and confidence.
- Gate: the researcher confirms relations and chooses which categories to follow; theoretical sampling of the next
  data.

## 3. Selective coding
- Researcher: chooses the core category and integrates the categories into a theory.
- Model: proposes candidate core categories with arguments for and against; drafts the storyline memo.
- Gate: the researcher chooses the core category and approves the storyline.

Throughout: constant comparison (segment with segment, code with code, category with category), saturation as the
stopping rule.
